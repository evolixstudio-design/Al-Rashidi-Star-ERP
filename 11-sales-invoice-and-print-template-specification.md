# Sales Invoice and Print Template Specification

## Application vs print language

Application screens use Hinglish. Printed customer invoice uses English + Arabic according to the approved Kuwait reference.

## Invoice data

- Invoice number
- Date
- Customer name/details
- Customer phone when required
- Item article number/description
- Quantity
- Unit price
- Line amount
- Invoice total
- Amount received
- Outstanding amount
- Payment status
- Due date if relevant
- Terms and signatures according to final reference

## Quantity print format

Use a clear quantity such as `2 Dozen 3 Pcs` and, if the layout allows, include `(27 Pcs)`.

## Print requirements

- A4 portrait target.
- English and Arabic labels/content in approved positions.
- Correct Arabic RTL rendering.
- K.D. / fils formatting where required.
- Logo and company identity configurable.
- Multi-page continuation for long invoices.

## Print actions

After sale completion show large actions:

- Print Invoice
- View Invoice
- WhatsApp Reminder when amount is still pending
- Done

## Final approval

Exact print layout requires the client's actual reference invoice and final sign-off before go-live.
