# Screen Requirements

## Home

Large greeting, five large quick-action buttons and a few summary cards. No dense analytics.

Quick actions:

1. New Sale / Invoice
2. Receive Shipment
3. Receive Payment
4. Add Expense
5. Search

## Receive Shipment — Step 1

Supplier, Shipment/Container No., Date, Supplier Reference, optional attachment. Large `Next` button.

## Receive Shipment — Step 2

Article Number, Product, Dozen, Pieces, Total Pieces, Purchase Price, Add Item. Article number gets first focus.

## Receive Shipment — Review

Show every product, quantity and purchase total. Primary action: `Receive & Add to Stock`.

## Current Stock

Columns must include:

Article | Product | Dozen + Pcs | Total Pcs | Price | Low Stock

## New Invoice — Customer

Customer search/create. Immediately show phone and existing outstanding.

## New Invoice — Products

Article-number search, available stock in both formats, Dozen and Pieces fields, selling price, line total.

## Invoice Review

Large total and three large payment choices: PAID, PARTIAL, PENDING.

## Pending Result

Show total, received, outstanding, due date and large `WhatsApp Reminder` action.

## Receive Payment

Search customer, show total outstanding and pending invoices, select invoice(s), enter amount, payment method and Save Payment.

## Customer Detail

Show phone, total sales, total received, total pending, pending invoice list, `New Sale` and `WhatsApp Reminder` buttons.

## Expense

Single simple form.

## Settings

Company identity, invoice numbering, invoice English/Arabic content, backup/admin preferences and user-account maintenance for the two owners.
