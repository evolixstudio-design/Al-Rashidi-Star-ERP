# Product Requirements Document

## Product goal

Give the two owners one reliable source of truth for stock, sales, purchases, pending customer payments, supplier payments, expenses and management reporting while keeping daily operation extremely easy.

## Locked user profile

- Exactly two normal application users.
- Both users are owners.
- Both have equal full access.
- Main age group: 50+.
- UI must be easy to understand after a short introduction.

## Core success outcomes

1. An owner can receive a China shipment and add actual received goods into stock without a separate manual stock-entry step.
2. Stock is stored internally as pieces but always displayed to the user as both Dozen + Pieces and Total Pieces.
3. An owner can create a direct invoice by selecting a customer, article number, quantity and price.
4. Posting an invoice deducts stock automatically.
5. Payment can be marked Paid, Partial or Pending.
6. A pending invoice provides a WhatsApp Reminder action with a prefilled message.
7. Later payments reduce the customer outstanding balance and update the invoice status automatically.
8. Dashboard and reports show current sales, purchases, expenses, stock and outstanding balances.
9. Daily software UI uses simple Hinglish.
10. Customer-facing invoice remains English + Arabic in the approved Kuwait layout.

## Main modules

1. Home / Dashboard
2. Products & Inventory
3. Purchases / Receive Shipment
4. Sales / Direct Invoice
5. Payments & Outstanding
6. Customers & Suppliers
7. Expenses
8. Reports
9. Settings & Audit

## UX requirement

The normal owner should mostly work through five large actions:

- New Sale / Invoice
- Receive Shipment
- Receive Payment
- Add Expense
- Search

Advanced reports and settings may exist in navigation but must not dominate the home screen.
