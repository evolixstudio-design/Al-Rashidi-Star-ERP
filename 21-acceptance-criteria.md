# Acceptance Criteria

Version 1 is accepted when all of the following are true:

1. Exactly two owner accounts can log in and both have full access.
2. Owner identity is recorded in audit history.
3. Products use unique article numbers.
4. Shipment receiving accepts Dozen and Pieces.
5. Posting a shipment automatically adds stock.
6. Current stock always displays both Dozen + Pieces and Total Pieces.
7. Article-number search works quickly during sales and purchases.
8. Direct invoice can be created without a sales order.
9. Posting invoice automatically deducts stock.
10. Invoice payment can be Paid, Partial or Pending.
11. Partial/pending amount creates correct customer outstanding.
12. Later payment reduces outstanding and updates invoice status.
13. Pending invoices with phone number provide a WhatsApp Reminder action with correct prefilled information.
14. Version 1 WhatsApp does not auto-send without the owner's action.
15. Expenses affect reports but not stock.
16. Dashboard and reports use only posted valid data.
17. Application daily UI is simple Hinglish.
18. Printed invoice is English + Arabic in the approved reference structure.
19. Posted transactions cannot be silently deleted/edited.
20. Both 50+ owners can complete shipment receipt, invoice, pending reminder and payment collection after a short guided introduction without help locating the next step.
