# Reporting Specification

## Dashboard summary

Keep the owner dashboard simple. Recommended cards:

- Sales
- Purchases
- Expenses
- Estimated Profit
- Customer Pending
- Supplier Pending
- Low Stock

Avoid dense charts in Version 1.

## Detailed reports

- Sales by date, customer, article number and product.
- Purchases by date, supplier, article number and shipment/container.
- Current stock.
- Stock movement.
- Low stock.
- Stock adjustments.
- Expenses by category/date.
- Customer ledger and outstanding.
- Supplier ledger and outstanding.
- Estimated profit and loss view.

## Inventory presentation

All stock reports show both:

- Dozen + Pieces
- Total Pieces

Example: `18 Dozen 7 Pcs | 223 Total Pcs`.

## Filters

Use simple date presets such as Today, This Week, This Month, Custom Date plus customer/supplier/product filters where relevant.

## Export

Reports may print and export to spreadsheet-compatible format.
