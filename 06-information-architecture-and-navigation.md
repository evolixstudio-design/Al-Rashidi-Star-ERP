# Information Architecture and Navigation

## Primary navigation

Keep the sidebar short and fixed:

1. Home
2. Sales
3. Purchases
4. Stock
5. Payments
6. Expenses
7. Customers
8. Suppliers
9. Reports
10. Settings

## Home quick actions

Use large buttons:

- New Sale / Invoice
- Receive Shipment
- Receive Payment
- Add Expense
- Search

## Sales submenu

- New Invoice
- Invoices
- Pending Payments

## Purchases submenu

- Receive Shipment
- Purchase History

A separate purchase order process can exist as an advanced/optional screen but should not be required for a normal shipment receipt.

## Stock submenu

- Current Stock
- Stock Movement
- Low Stock
- Stock Adjustment

## Navigation rules for 50+ users

- No deep nested menus.
- No icon-only navigation.
- Always show text beside icons.
- Use the same Back, Save, Review, Post and Print positions on similar screens.
- Keep one obvious primary action per screen.
- Use Hinglish labels with familiar business English words.
