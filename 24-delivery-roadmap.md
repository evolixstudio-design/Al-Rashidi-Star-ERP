# Delivery Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Foundation

- Project setup.
- Database.
- Two owner accounts.
- Hinglish UI shell.
- Company settings.
- Audit log.
- Product/category master.

## Phase 2 — Inventory and purchasing

- Stock ledger.
- Dozen + Pieces conversion/display.
- Suppliers.
- Receive Shipment workflow.
- Opening stock import.

## Phase 3 — Sales and payments

- Customers.
- Direct invoice.
- Automatic stock deduction.
- Paid / Partial / Pending status.
- Outstanding balances.
- Receive Payment.
- WhatsApp reminder.

## Phase 4 — Expenses and reports

- Expense entry.
- Dashboard.
- Sales/purchase/stock/outstanding/profit reports.

## Phase 5 — Printing and launch

- English + Arabic invoice template.
- Client invoice sign-off.
- Real data import.
- Backup setup.
- Two-owner training.
- Parallel testing with current manual records.
- Go-live.

## Delivery rule

Do not move to the next major phase until the current phase has passed automated tests and a practical manual walkthrough by the owners.
