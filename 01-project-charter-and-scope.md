# Project Charter and Scope

## Product name

Rashidi Traders ERP — Kuwait Trading, Inventory, Billing and Payment Tracking System.

## Purpose

Build a very simple trading and inventory system for two business owners aged 50+. The system replaces paper/manual work for shipments, purchasing, stock, invoicing, pending payments, expenses and business reports.

## Primary users

There are only two users. Both are owners and both have the same full access. There are no staff roles, department roles, manager approvals or restricted employee screens in Version 1.

The system still records which owner created, changed, posted, printed, cancelled or reversed each transaction.

## Primary daily workflow

Shipment arrives from China -> Receive Shipment / Purchase Entry -> Add received goods to stock -> Sell products -> Create invoice -> Mark Paid / Partial / Pending -> If pending, open WhatsApp reminder -> Receive later payment -> Invoice becomes Paid.

## In scope for Version 1

- Owner login for two accounts.
- Simple home dashboard.
- Product and category master.
- Purchase / shipment receiving.
- Automatic stock ledger.
- Inventory display in Dozen + Pieces and Total Pieces.
- Direct sales invoice.
- Customer and supplier records.
- Paid, Partial and Pending payment tracking.
- WhatsApp reminder action for pending customer payments.
- Expense entry.
- Customer and supplier balances.
- Sales, purchase, stock, expense, outstanding and profit reports.
- Hinglish application UI.
- English + Arabic customer-facing invoice template based on the supplied reference.
- Audit history.
- Data import for opening stock, customers and suppliers.

## Out of scope for Version 1

E-commerce, payroll, barcode hardware, advanced journal accounting, multi-branch consolidation, automatic WhatsApp Business API sending, online payment gateways and government filing integrations.

## Product principle

The interface must feel easier than a traditional ERP. Every common job should use large text, large buttons, plain wording, minimal choices and a clear next step. The system may be powerful internally, but the owners should not need accounting or technical knowledge to operate it.
